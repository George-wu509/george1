Introducing [LoopX](https://www.linkedin.com/company/loopx-ai/) Data Fusion–Based Mapping & Localization Technology  
I’m excited to share our latest innovation — a system that fuses LiDAR, satellite imagery, GPS, and inertial measurement units with advanced multimodal Simultaneous Localization and Mapping algorithms. The result? One seamless 3D map and geolocation tracking that works across both surface and underground environments.  
Unlike existing solutions on the market that either rely solely on GNSS, depend on expensive Inertial Navigation Systems that require precise GNSS references at the surface, drift during harsh underground maneuvering, and cannot even start from underground without that reference, or require hundreds of costly underground beacons, LoopX delivers precise localization both above and below ground, along with globally geo-referenced 3D mapping — all in a single platform.  
This technology enables continuous situational awareness that can be integrated into Fleet Management Systems (FMS) or Integrated Remote Operation Centres (IROC), supporting safer operations, smarter planning, and a clear path to autonomy in mining.  
👉 Contact us to see this technology live at [NORCAT](https://www.linkedin.com/company/norcat/)’s Underground Mine. It is also currently being trialed with two production mines.
