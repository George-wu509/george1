Excited to announce that my first-authored paper "CXReasonBench: A Benchmark for Evaluating Structured Diagnostic Reasoning in Chest X-rays" has been accepted to [hashtag#NeurIPS2025](https://www.linkedin.com/search/results/all/?keywords=%23neurips2025&origin=HASH_TAG_FROM_FEED) (Dataset and Benchmark Track) as a Spotlight presentation 🎉  
  
🔎 Why this matters  
Large Vision-Language Models (LVLMs) have shown remarkable progress in medical AI, but most benchmarks only evaluate the final diagnostic answer. This leaves open the question: do models actually perform clinically meaningful reasoning?  
  
💡 Our contributions  
🔹 CheXStruct: an automated pipeline that derives structured reasoning steps directly from chest X-rays (segmentation, anatomical landmarks, diagnostic measurements, clinical thresholds).  
🔹 CXReasonBench: a benchmark built on top of CheXStruct, designed to evaluate structured diagnostic reasoning in a transparent, fine-grained way.  
  
📊 Highlights  
🔹 18,988 QA pairs across 12 diagnostic tasks and 1,200 cases  
🔹 Multi-path, multi-stage evaluation (including visual grounding and diagnostic measurements)  
🔹 Evaluation of 10 LVLMs → even the strongest models still struggle with structured reasoning and generalization
