• Develop, validate, and execute state-of-the-art ==imaging software simulation tools== to enable novel ==camera HW explorations==, ==machine learning dataset generation==, and product architecture tasks for consumer products. 開發、驗證並執行先進的影像軟體模擬工具，以支援新型相機硬體探索、機器學習資料集產生以及消費性產品的產品架構任務。
